# Audio Checklist

- Asset inventory and event ownership are complete.
- Loops are seamless and do not click.
- Win tiers are distinguishable without excessive loudness.
- Mute and volume controls are persistent and accessible.
- Polyphony, ducking, and interruption behavior are bounded.
- Fast play, replay, reduced intensity, and background-tab behavior are tested.
- Mobile memory, decoding, and package-size budgets pass.
- Source ownership and licenses are recorded.
